My Favorite Recipe

Tofu Musubi

Ingredients

  • 1 block of extra firm tofu
  • 1 cup sushi rice
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon fresh ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon honey
  • Optional: 1/2 teaspoon red chili flakes
  • Sushi Nori
  • Plastic wrap

Instructions

  1. Prepare your sushi rice by adding 1 cup sushi rice and 1 cup water to a sauce pan and bring to a boil, once it starts boiling turn it to low and cover for 12 to 15 minutes until rice is fully cooked. Remove from heat and let stand for another 12 to 15 minutes then add 1/4 cup rice vinegar, 1/2 teaspoon sugar and fluff the rice using a fork.
  2. Next, Prepare your tofu by pressing it for 15 to 20 minutes to remove extra liquid. Then cut tofu into vertical pieces to create little tofu patties.
  3. To prepare the sauce, in a small bowl, add 1/2 cup soy sauce, 1 tablespoon brown sugar, 1/2 teaspoon honey, 1 clove of chopped garlic, 1/2 teaspoon of chopped ginger, and red chili flakes if you like it spicy. Mix together until combined.
  4. In a pan, add a little olive oil and cook your tofu on medium heat on both sides until they are golden brown.
  5. Once your tofu is cooked, add the sauce into the pan and let simmer until sauce is caramelized.
  6. Time to assemble, take a sheet of plastic wrap and in the center of the sheet put a scoop of your sushi rice, about 1/4 cup. Using the plastic wrap, shape the rice into a rectangle that is about the same size as your tofu patties.
  7. Once you have your rice shaped, unwrap it from the plastic wrap and place one of your tofu patties on top of it.
  8. Take your sushi nori and cut it into long strips that are about 1 inch wide. Once you have your strips, take your rice and tofu and gently wrap the nori around it horizontally.
  9. If you are not going to eat them right away, leave off the nori until you are ready to eat them so it doesn't get soggy.
