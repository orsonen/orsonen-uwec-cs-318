Olivia Rodrigo

A short analysis of Oliva Rodrigo's

You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love

Track List

drop dead

A song about having a crush that is so intense that it makes you dizzy
and overdramatic

stupid song

You love someone so much that there are no words to express the feelings
you have towards them

honeybee

A mature love song that talks about how fragile this love is with
undertones of deep devotion and the fear of loss

maggots for brains

A song about the intense lovesickness a person can make you feel and how
anytime spent away from them is painful

u + me = <3

You are so in love with this person that you want to move really fast and
you don't care what anyone else thinks

my way

When your jealous ex is making himself known in your new relationship but
your love is worth him getting jealous over

purple

The emotional turning point of the record that shifts from a lovesickness
to an emotional sickness

the cure

The painful realization that the love of your partner is not going to fix
your mental health

begged

Realizing that you have to beg for affection and basic emotional needs in
your relationship

what's wrong with me

Going to the doctor to try and figure out why you are so sad and depressed
only to realize that your relationship is toxic

less

The final attempt to try and save your dying relationship and wishing your
partner loved you less so it would make the breakup less painful

expectations

Growing out of the relationship and realizing your worth

cigarette smoke

A blunt and honest breakup song driven by regret and resentment that are
linguring after the heartbreak
